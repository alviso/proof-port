What was checked and what wasn't

Checked, for every case: the error messages (by text) and every row of ZRET_T_ARTICLE, ZRET_T_ART_VAR after the old screen and after the new app, each from the same starting data. No calls into the old transaction were found in the port or its driver.

Not checked:

  • Batch input proves rules, not display. What a screen shows (names, greyed fields, titles) is only seen through what it accepts and what it saves.
  • A success message sent just before the transaction ends never reaches batch input, so for saves the rows are the evidence, not that message.
  • Batch input stops at the first error, so retyping after a refusal on the same screen is not tested.
  • F4 value help does not run under batch input.
  • Timestamps written by either side are compared as present or empty, not by value.
  • Messages are compared by their text when they are errors. Warnings and information messages are not compared.
